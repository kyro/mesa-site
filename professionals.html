A notebook that fits your work.

We’re exploring reusable custom templates, goal-setting workflows, MCP, and broader syncing. The current calendar connection reads Google Calendar events; it is not a general two-way sync service.

A few things
to know.

What does the meeting demo show?

Notes, a transcript of a synthetic sample recording, and AI-generated actions and decisions. The transcript and summaries were produced by the app’s real services.

Can Mesa connect to my work tools?

The prototype has a read-only Google Calendar connection. MCP and broader integrations are concepts we are evaluating, not available features shown here.

What happens to recordings?

The prototype stores meeting audio with its page and uses cloud services for transcription and AI summaries. Final product controls and data policies are still being developed. Use sample material when evaluating this prototype.
