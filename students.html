Exploring what’s next

Bring the course to the page.

We’re exploring PDF download and annotation, along with a more structured practice-and-feedback experience. These are concepts in development; PDF import and a dedicated study system are not shown in this demo.

A few things
to know.

Can I import lecture slides or PDFs?

PDF import and document annotation are being explored. This prototype demonstration uses a study page created in Mesa.

Does it grade my work?

The demo uses Mesa’s AI companion to generate a question and comment on an answer. It is not a validated grading system, and it can make mistakes. Check explanations against your course materials.

Is this an iPad app or an e-ink notebook?

Mesa is being developed as a small e-ink notebook. The video shows the current experience running as an iPad prototype; it does not demonstrate final hardware performance.
