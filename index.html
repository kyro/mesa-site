Fair questions.

Is Mesa a tablet?

No. It does a few things well: writing by hand, cleaning it up, transcribing, and finding things later. It doesn't browse, it doesn't ping, and it doesn't want to be your phone.

Does it work without wifi?

Yes. Writing, sketching, templates and dictation all run on the notebook itself. Search across everything, the cleaner clean-up and the backup use the cloud when you have it, and wait when you don't.

Who owns my notes?

You do. Every page is also a plain text file with a title, a few tags and a summary. Open them anywhere, hand them to whatever AI you like, or walk away with them. Your handwriting is yours too: a small file of strokes you drew.

Is there a subscription, and when does it ship?

You don't need a subscription to write, draw, dictate, or use templates. Pricing for the cloud features will be published at launch. Mesa ships in the summer of 2027.
