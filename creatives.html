Exploring what’s next

More ways to follow the idea.

Sketch editing and built-in creative pages are in the prototype. We’re exploring more templates and cleanup workflows. The demo shows an addition to a drawing; it does not promise that every kind of ink can be automatically reshaped.

A few things
to know.

Does it replace my drawing?

The recorded demo adds new lines beside an original sketch. You choose the instruction and can undo the result. The prototype’s editing behavior varies by operation and the kind of ink on the page.

Which creative templates can I try?

The prototype includes writing, layout, music, and sketch-oriented tools. We’re using these pages to learn which creative workflows people most want Mesa to support.

Is the video a mockup?

The interface video is captured from the real iPad prototype, using an original sample sketch and live AI output. Captured actions play at their original speed, with a closing pause to see the result. Product imagery illustrates the planned hardware.
