Exploring what’s next

A reading life, with room in the margins.

Reading is part of the life we imagine for Mesa. EPUB support and book annotation are still being explored. The current demonstration shows the working journal experience; a reading preview is included in the campaign concepts.

A few things
to know.

Can I read my books on Mesa?

EPUB reading is part of the concept we are exploring. A book reader is not implemented in the current iPad prototype, so the recorded demo focuses on journaling.

Does journaling come with goals or streaks?

The page is yours to use at your own pace. This experience is centered on writing and returning to your thoughts, with a timer available when you want one.

When can I get one?

Mesa is in development. This preview helps us learn what people want from a small e-ink notebook. Availability, pricing, and the final feature set are still being shaped.
